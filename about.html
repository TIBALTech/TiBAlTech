WHY TIBAL?

The Three Pillars of TiBAL Tech

Our brand name honors the iconic figures who laid the foundation for every digital interaction we enjoy today.

TI

Tim Berners-Lee

Inventor of the World Wide Web

In 1989, Tim Berners-Lee invented the World Wide Web—creating the first web browser, HTML, HTTP, and URLs. His vision opened human communication to a global scale.

Our Connection: We connect people and ideas through open, responsive, and thoughtfully engineered digital experiences.
B

Charles Babbage

Father of the Computer

Charles Babbage conceptualized and designed the first mechanical computing engines—the Difference Engine and the Analytical Engine—proving that human thought and calculation could be mechanized.

Our Connection: We engineer disciplined digital architectures, ensuring every system runs with precision and structural clarity.
AL

Alan Turing

Father of Modern Computing & AI

Alan Turing formulated the mathematical concepts of algorithm, computation, and the universal machine, breaking foundational ciphers and pioneering artificial intelligence.

Our Connection: We apply logical rigor, smart problem-solving, and continuous exploration of future backend solutions.
